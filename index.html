How to play

Stir the harbor. Sound the horn. Send her to sea.

Cast off and the tug pulls her off the quay · Space sounds the horn · steer the yellow skiff with WASD or arrows, or tap the water · if someone slips into the jelly, fetch them and drop them at the pier ladder · drag the water to pull the jelly · drag the block's side to tip it · drag empty space to orbit, scroll to zoom

This piece needs WebGL2. Try a recent version of Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
